## Question

What reproducible walkthrough coverage and evidence will later demonstrate each of Phase 1's five approved acceptance criteria, and what unresolved findings must prevent its preview journey from being declared complete?

Decide how the future evaluation will cover anonymous discovery and study, coherent teaching, revision-correct source references and claim distinctions, correct and incorrect exercise feedback, actionable local validation without implied verification, and traceable creator approval. Identify who records actual versions, check results, and outstanding findings. Keep implementation checks, creator publication approval, optional formative learner observations, and human evaluation distinct; do not invent Phase 6 success thresholds or infer educational effectiveness from readiness. At this map's end, identify the route into a separately authorized specification effort, without creating specifications or implementation tickets and without recording planned checks as performed checks.
