## Answer

Resolved through live exchange with the creator on 2026-10-20. The creator confirmed walkthrough coverage, evidence ownership, completion blockers, and the complete shared understanding. This is an evaluation and handoff planning decision; no planned check is recorded as performed.

### Reproducible coverage of the five acceptance criteria

The later evaluation must provide reproducible steps, expected results, and actual observations for one connected journey covering:

1. **Anonymous discovery and study:** enter through the catalog, understand prerequisites and limited preview scope, navigate and study the five-lesson progression, and attempt practice without registration or payment.
2. **Coherent teaching:** trace the stated problem through concepts, connected actual source behavior, the architectural trade-off, and practice. Check that the sequence teaches the workflow rather than presenting a directory listing.
3. **Source and evidence integrity:** inspect exact source references against the identified fixed revision and their explanations. Check evidence distinctions, uncertainty, educational reconstruction, and criticism without unsupported history or defect claims.
4. **Browser and local practice:** try every prepared exercise choice, including correct and incorrect responses, and check intended feedback, hints, and retries. Actually perform the local assignment using its observable validation guidance to demonstrate that the instructions are actionable. This evaluator execution does not establish platform verification of learners' local work or broader proficiency.
5. **External publication traceability:** trace the distributed release to its source revision, rights basis, technical and educational review evidence, and explicit creator publication approval for the exact package and scope.

### Evidence record and responsibility

The creator owns the evaluation record. Agents may assist with preparation and technical checks. Record the actual course release, full source revision, content commit, package checksum, environment, evaluated address, evaluator, date, steps, expected and observed results, and outstanding findings.

Keep automated implementation checks, human walkthrough observations, creator publication approval, and optional formative learner observations separately identifiable. Unperformed checks remain pending. Automated passes and guide readiness do not establish human acceptance; human evaluation remains pending until actual observations are reported. Optional formative observations do not establish measured learning effectiveness.

### Findings that prevent Phase 1 completion

Missing required evidence or a failure that materially obstructs or misrepresents the stated anonymous learning journey blocks completion. This includes broken anonymous access, materially incoherent teaching, incorrect source references or unsupported claims, wrong exercise feedback, unusable local validation guidance, missing rights/review/approval, and a mismatch between approved and distributed packages.

Minor findings may remain only with a recorded rationale and any necessary learner-visible limitation. Preserve the already agreed publication and withdrawal requirements in [Define the external creator publication process](07-define-creator-publication-process.md) and [Choose the minimum content and delivery approach](08-choose-content-and-delivery-approach.md). Phase 1 delivery completion requires the approved preview, recorded results across all five criteria, and no unresolved finding preventing the journey. Acceptance of this bounded journey does not establish broader proficiency or learning effectiveness; no Phase 6 thresholds are invented here.

### Route into subsequent specification

The Wayfinder decision map is complete for a separately authorized specification effort. That effort will use the resolved decisions to define product behavior, content requirements, and reproducible evaluation procedures. Carry forward pending source verification, review, publication approval, and actual walkthrough results. Revisit repository-specific surprises if deeper inspection exposes a concrete issue; possible future discoveries do not constitute an unresolved decision at this handoff.

This session creates no specification, implementation ticket, application, prepared course asset, deployment, or publication approval. No human walkthrough or learning-effectiveness result is established by closing this map.
