## Question

What minimal creator-operated external process will connect preparation, rights checks, technical and educational review, explicit approval, stable release identity, and preview publication with traceable evidence?

Decide ownership and the evidence needed for review of objectives, curriculum coherence, exercises, source references, and claim sufficiency; how a publication decision identifies exactly the content release and fixed source revision; and how unresolved findings prevent distribution. How will drafts, withdrawn approval, major corrections, revised educational content, and eventual preview expansion respect stable releases? Preserve the already approved versioning rules and future association of learner records without designing those records. Decide the process and record responsibilities without creating a dashboard, granting approval, or publishing content.

## Answer

Resolved through live exchange with the creator on 2026-10-20. All six recommendations and the complete shared understanding were explicitly confirmed. This establishes the external publication process for later specification; it grants no content-release approval and records no publication execution.

### Ownership and preparation

The creator owns preparation, final publication approval, and distribution verification. Preparation and review happen outside the running application, without a dedicated in-app authoring or publishing dashboard. Agents may assist with preparation and separate technical and educational review passes. Record who performed each review and their findings. The creator may perform both reviews; an independent reviewer is optional. Agent output and automated check results do not substitute for explicit creator approval.

### Candidate review record and distribution blockers

Maintain one external review record per candidate release, linking the exact content, rights basis, source references, actual verification results, educational review, and outstanding findings. Technical and educational review together cover technical correctness, objectives, curriculum and lesson coherence, exercise answers and prepared feedback, local-assignment guidance, source-reference accuracy, and claim sufficiency. Preserve the source/evidence requirements in [Establish the fixed source and evidence basis](02-establish-source-evidence-basis.md), including third-party rights review and recorded behavior verification using an explicitly isolated disposable database. Inspected tests are not passing execution evidence.

Missing rights, missing required behavior verification, and material technical or educational findings block distribution, including preview distribution. Minor findings may remain only with a recorded rationale and any necessary learner-visible limitation. A finding's treatment must not hide material uncertainty or imply stronger evidence than exists.

### Exact approval target

Identify the educational release separately from its fixed source revision. The approval record includes a readable course-release identifier, immutable content reference, full source commit, approved preview scope, and dated explicit creator approval. Approval applies to that exact content snapshot and scope; it does not silently carry over to another snapshot, even if both teach the same source revision. This process decision does not select a storage format, release-naming syntax, or delivery technology; the minimum content and delivery decision follows separately.

### Draft changes, corrections, and preview expansion

Draft edits invalidate affected review results. Recheck affected evidence and obtain approval for the exact final snapshot before distribution. Published teaching content is stable: major corrections, revised educational content, a newer source revision, and expanded preview curricula require a separate course-release identity, review, and approval. Earlier releases remain identifiable rather than being silently replaced. Future learner records stay associated with the version actually studied, without automatic transfer or synchronization; this decision does not design those records.

### Withdrawal and restoration

Withdrawn approval or a newly discovered blocking issue stops distribution of the affected release. Remove access to its teaching materials and show a withdrawal notice at its existing entry point. Retain its release identity, evidence, and withdrawal reason internally. A replacement points to a separately approved release. Restoring an unchanged release requires recorded resolution of the blocking issue and renewed creator approval; changed teaching material follows the separate-release rule.

### Distribution check and actual outcome

Before distribution, the creator confirms that the content snapshot, source revision, preview scope, review findings, and approval match. After distribution, the creator records the date, location, and whether the actual distributed material matches the approved release. A mismatch stops distribution until corrected. Neither a planned check nor this process agreement counts as an actual successful distribution result.
