# 11: Distribute the creator-approved preview

**What to build:** Invited evaluators can anonymously open a stable release-specific preview of exactly the package the creator approved. The external publication record demonstrates agreement among final content, rights/review evidence, approved scope, uploaded package, and the actual distributed location.

**Blocked by:** 10 — Prepare the reviewed, exact course-release package.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Present the already complete candidate, review findings/dispositions, final content identity, source revision, build environment, complete artifact checksum, and proposed preview scope to the creator. Obtain and record dated explicit creator publication approval of those exact identities and scope before uploading teaching material.
- [ ] Treat missing approval as pending: planning approval, approved implementation tickets, automated checks, and agent review do not authorize course distribution. Prepare all reviewable work before requesting this required final approval.
- [ ] Immediately before distribution, verify candidate, rights, verification/review evidence, findings, package, scope, and approval still agree. A new blocking finding or any mismatch stops distribution until corrected and approved as required.
- [ ] Manually upload the approved complete package using the rehearsed prebuilt procedure without rebuilding. Keep the dedicated project disconnected from Git with automatic publication disabled, and record evidence that uploaded package and checksum match the approved artifact.
- [ ] Serve the preview at the stable release-specific learner address. Verify actual anonymous access, protection settings, noindex controls, direct lesson/section access, and required content/assets at the hosted address in a fresh browser.
- [ ] Record the actual distribution date and location, course release, immutable content reference, full fixed source revision, artifact identity, approval reference, scope, and observed package agreement. Do not describe a provider production target or an unlisted preview as public V1 launch.
- [ ] Make the release address available to the creator for invited evaluation. Do not send invitations or messages to other people without explicit authorization. Explain that the address can be forwarded and noindex is not access control.
- [ ] Preserve the rehearsed withdrawal inventory and procedure for the actual release. Withdrawn approval or a blocking finding stops distribution, replaces the stable entry with a notice, removes every affected hosted copy, and verifies former material URLs cease serving it while internal evidence is retained.
- [ ] Preserve stable educational releases: revised teaching, major corrections, a new source revision, or expanded preview curriculum require separate course-release identity, review, and approval. Unchanged restoration requires recorded resolution and renewed approval; do not silently repoint an old approved release to edited teaching.
- [ ] Review actual distribution evidence and complete the global ticket-walkthrough handoff with hosted preflight results, reproducible evaluator steps, and the creator's first action. Human evaluation remains pending until observations are reported; availability alone does not complete Phase 1.
