# 12: Evaluate the complete Phase 1 journey

**What to build:** The creator has a reproducible evaluation of the available approved preview against all five Phase 1 roadmap criteria, with actual preflight evidence, reported human observations, and traceable finding dispositions. Prepare the guide and first action autonomously, then keep human observations and Phase 1 completion explicitly pending until the required evidence exists.

**Blocked by:** 11 — Distribute the creator-approved preview.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Maintain a creator-owned evaluation record identifying actual course release, full fixed source revision, immutable content reference or commit, artifact checksum, environment, evaluated address, evaluator, date, reproducible steps, expected/observed results, and outstanding findings. Retain per-ticket checkpoints from earlier handoffs.
- [ ] Criterion 1 — Anonymous discovery and study: guide a fresh anonymous visitor from release entry through fit/prerequisites/scope, Start learning, navigation and direct lesson/section loading, and practice. Record observations that no account, payment, or mandatory pass gate intervenes.
- [ ] Criterion 2 — Coherent teaching: follow all five lessons from the repeated-`/start` problem through connected concepts/source, state preservation, bounded trade-off, and practice. Collect actual observations about the diagram, explained self-checks, and decreasing guidance alongside named technical/educational review findings; page existence is not coherence evidence.
- [ ] Criterion 3 — Source and evidence integrity: compare selected excerpts/references and explanations against the full fixed revision and actual behavior checks. Inspect claim classifications, uncertainty, material gaps, and reconstruction framing. Record any unsupported history or defect claim as a blocking finding until resolved.
- [ ] Criterion 4 — Browser practice: observe intended prepared feedback and evidence links for correct/incorrect choices, mixed predictions/reasoning, unanswered/partial/unsupported responses, hints, separate explanations, edits/resubmission, retries, and reset. Check reference access and the absence of score/proficiency claims or saved results.
- [ ] Criterion 4 — Local practice: actually perform the assignment using the published fixed-revision setup, baseline, name/fallback requirements, regression failures before change, passing checks afterward, and scope/diff review. Record environment, commands, and observed results, keeping learner local results and self-reviewed explanations visibly unverified by the platform.
- [ ] Criterion 5 — External publication traceability: trace the hosted preview to exact release/content/source identities, rights basis, behavior evidence, technical/educational review, complete approved package/checksum, build environment, dated explicit creator approval/scope, actual distribution location, and package agreement.
- [ ] Perform authorized preflight checks and record actual results, then use the global ticket-walkthrough skill in the implementation chat to prepare feature-by-feature evaluation steps and give the creator the first concrete action. Preparation and automated passes do not establish human acceptance or add a new implementation approval gate.
- [ ] Record human evaluation as pending until the creator/evaluator reports observations. Attribute reported observations accurately and keep them distinct from agent preflight, publication approval, implementation checks, and optional formative observations; do not invent results or close acceptance from guide readiness.
- [ ] Triage and resolve or disposition findings with evidence and repeat affected checks/observations. Broken access, incoherent teaching, incorrect references/claims/feedback, unusable local guidance, missing rights/review/approval, or package mismatch block Phase 1 delivery completion. Minor findings require recorded rationale and any necessary learner-facing limitation.
- [ ] Record Phase 1 delivery complete only when the approved preview is available, all five criteria have reproducible walkthroughs and actual recorded results including required human observations, and no material blocking finding remains. This ticket and human evaluation remain incomplete while required observations are missing; required creator publication approval remains a separate release gate.
- [ ] State the practical limits of acceptance: bounded practice, local self-report, and optional formative observations do not establish broader independent proficiency or measured learning effectiveness. Do not expand into accounts, saved records, adaptation, formal cohort validation, or public V1 launch.
