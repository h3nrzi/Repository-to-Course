# 06: Practice the new-Buyer prediction

**What to build:** A learner can enter Apply the understanding and complete a reference-assisted new-Buyer prediction with immediate, separately evaluated feedback for state, reply behavior, and supporting reasoning. Deliver the complete interaction for this first scenario so later scenarios extend prepared content rather than requiring another exercise implementation.

**Blocked by:** 03 — Follow the connected /start workflow.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Publish Apply the understanding as a freely accessible lesson with stable lesson/section links and practice links back to the relevant lessons, recurring diagram, and source excerpts. Keep the recommended order driven by content as other lessons become available.
- [ ] Anchor the new-Buyer scenario to the original fixed-revision behavior: one Buyer and Wallet, zero Available Balance, generic new-Buyer welcome, Wallet-creation confirmation, initial balance, and Buyer menu. The later personalization assignment does not change this answer contract.
- [ ] Offer separate provided selections for Buyer/Wallet state, reply behavior, and supporting explanation. Evaluate each submitted part independently using prepared accepted choices and per-choice feedback with relevant source references.
- [ ] Confirm correct reasoning and explain the particular misconception for incorrect choices. A correct state/reply prediction and incorrect explanation receive distinct mixed feedback rather than one aggregate judgment.
- [ ] Fully unanswered and partially answered submissions preserve missing parts as unanswered while evaluating completed parts. Invalid or unsupported values prompt reselection without being marked educationally incorrect.
- [ ] Keep selections editable after feedback, require explicit resubmission after edits, and ensure old feedback is not presented as an evaluation of an edited answer. Support unlimited retries and a fresh-attempt reset that clears selections and attempt-specific feedback/reveals.
- [ ] Offer optional source-directed hints and a separately revealed worked explanation. Revealing an explanation does not fill missing selections or establish proficiency; references remain accessible without a timer or pass gate.
- [ ] Implement browser-only unsaved state through a Client Component, with prepared data compiled at build time. State that results are not saved; provide no overall score, proficiency award, saved history, or remote evaluation endpoint.
- [ ] Extend the structured content contract and build validation for stable scenario/part/choice identities, allowed and accepted choices, complete per-choice feedback, evidence links, hints, and worked explanations. Reject inconsistent relationships and incomplete required data.
- [ ] Through the built site's visible controls, check every supported choice for all three parts and relevant feedback/source links, mixed reasoning, unanswered/unsupported responses, hints/reveals, edits/resubmission, retries/reset, and absence of saved results. Establish expected outcomes independently of the evaluator under test.
- [ ] Complete required checks and review, then the global ticket-walkthrough handoff with actual preflight evidence and the creator's first action. Human evaluation remains pending until observations are reported.
